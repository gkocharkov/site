About

I am an economist at the Bundesbank Research Centre in Frankfurt. From 2024 to 2026 I was at the European Central Bank, working on the Consumer Expectations Survey. Before joining the Bundesbank in 2019, I was Assistant Professor of Economics at Goethe University Frankfurt and at the University of Konstanz.

My research uses quantitative macroeconomic models and household survey data to study fiscal policy, taxation, housing and wealth, and family economics. I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid.

Since 2023 I have been a member of the Bulgarian Council for Economic Analyses, an advisory body to the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Bulgaria.
